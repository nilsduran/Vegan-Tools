# Other funding options (besides NLnet)

*Checked 2026-11-15. "Open" means the official page showed applications or sign-ups as possible on that date. Re-check before applying.*

| # | Option | Type | Status (2026-11-15) | Fit | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [EA Funds: Animal Welfare Fund](https://funds.effectivealtruism.org/funds/animal-welfare) ([apply](https://funds.effectivealtruism.org/apply-for-funding)) | Grant (individuals and organisations worldwide) | **Open**: "Always open to applications" | **Medium.** It is the only listed funder with an animal-ethics mission, and it has no GenAI ban like NLnet's. However, its stated 3-year priorities are farmed animals (cage-free, fish and shrimp, slaughter), invertebrates, wild animals and the Global South. A consumer app has to argue measurable impact on animals, not convenience. | Low to medium. The form takes about an hour. A one-page impact and metrics case helps. |
| 2 | [Liberapay](https://liberapay.com/about/) | Recurring donations | **Open**: sign-up available | **High** on values: a non-profit, takes no cut (only payment-processor fees), no ads or tracking. Income is small until there is an audience. | Very low. Create a profile and link it from `/about` and the README. |
| 3 | [GitHub Sponsors](https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/receiving-sponsorships-through-github-sponsors/about-github-sponsors-for-open-source-contributors) | Recurring and one-off donations | Open to people in supported regions (check that Spain is listed at sign-up) | **Medium-high.** No fees on sponsorships from personal accounts, and the repo is already on GitHub. Reaches developers more than people looking for vegan food. | Low. Profile, payout details and tax form. |
| 4 | [Open Source Collective](https://oscollective.org/) on Open Collective ([criteria](https://docs.oscollective.org/getting-started/acceptance-criteria)) | Fiscal host plus transparent public budget | **Open**: applications accepted | **Medium.** Gives a public ledger that suits the zero-tracking, transparency values, and lets you receive grants and donations without your own legal entity. Requires the repo to live in a **GitHub organisation, not a personal account**, and an OSI/FSF-approved licence (AGPL qualifies). A host fee applies; check the terms of fiscal sponsorship. | Medium. Move the repo to an organisation, preferably with two admins. |
| 5 | [FLOSS/fund](https://floss.fund/) ([FAQ](https://floss.fund/faq/)) | Grant ($10k minimum, up to $100k a year per project) | **Open**: submit a `funding.json` manifest URL. Reviewed at the end of each quarter. | **Low for now.** The FAQ says very new or little-used projects are "not currently considered", and Vegan Tools started in July 2026. Worth applying once there is real usage or the classifier library or dataset is reused elsewhere. A `funding.json` also lists the project in its public directory. | Low. Write and host `funding.json`. |

## Checked and not open now

- **[Animal Charity Evaluators: Movement Grants](https://animalcharityevaluators.org/movement-grants/).** Round 12 closed on 18 October 2026, and no date is announced for round 13. Grants focus on advocacy organisations, with special interest in Asia and Africa. Watch for the 2027 call.
- **NLnet: NGI Zero Commons Fund.** Closed for good on 1 June 2026. See `nlnet-application-draft.md` for Restack and the open call instead.
- **Catalan-language grants (Generalitat, Política Lingüística).** I found no open 2026 call for apps or digital tools. The Beques Propulsió (for digital creators) ran in 2025; a 2026 round is unconfirmed. Worth checking the DOGC, because the full Catalan localisation is a genuine fit.

## Suggested order

1. **Now, under an hour:** Liberapay and GitHub Sponsors profiles, linked from `/about`.
2. **Before 3 January 2027:** decide on NLnet (see the open questions in `nlnet-application-draft.md`).
3. **Any time:** an EA Animal Welfare Fund application if you can make a credible animal-impact case.
4. **Later, once there is usage:** an Open Source Collective organisation and FLOSS/fund.
